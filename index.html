Named Orthodox Bible. The text is the public-domain World English Bible with deuterocanon, listed in Orthodox Study Bible book order (Kingdoms, Wisdom, Sirach, Maccabees, Psalm 151). The printed OSB uses the NKJV and the St. Athanasius Academy Septuagint, which cannot be copied here. Book introductions, “How to read,” and verse notes are original Orthodox notes in a study-Bible pattern (preamble, typology, cross-references, footers) — not the copyrighted OSB apparatus. Hours follow the Alexandrian Agpeya. Tewahedo and Coptic tabs use the shared Alexandrian civil calendar with each church’s commemorations. Daily passages are a home cycle, not the parish Gitsawē or Katameros. Enoch, Jubilees, and the Meqabyan books of the Tewahedo canon are not in this English file yet. Bookmarks, highlights, and comments stay on this phone.
