Named Orthodox Bible. The text is the public-domain World English Bible with deuterocanon, listed in Orthodox Study Bible book order (Kingdoms, Wisdom, Sirach, Maccabees, Psalm 151). The printed OSB uses the NKJV and the St. Athanasius Academy Septuagint, which cannot be copied here. Hours follow the Alexandrian Agpeya. Tewahedo and Coptic tabs use the shared Alexandrian civil calendar with each church’s commemorations. Daily passages are a home cycle, not the parish Gitsawē or Katameros. Enoch, Jubilees, and the Meqabyan books of the Tewahedo canon are not in this English file yet. Progress stays on this phone.
